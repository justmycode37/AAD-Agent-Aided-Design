# Snap fits

Unmeasured candidate guidance. Specify whether the joint is permanent or reusable, the mating geometry, insertion and release direction, material, process, temperature, cycle target, and acceptable assembly force.

Choose a snap form that suits the interface, then review beam or ring deflection, root transition, lead-in, undercut, and release access. Deflection and fatigue depend on geometry, resin, manufacturing orientation, and environment; use material-specific guidance instead of a universal beam ratio.

AIAD has no strain, retention-force, fatigue, or snap-fit evaluator. The injection draft screen does not detect undercuts or mold release. A geometrically valid hook is not proof that it assembles or survives.

Source: [Protolabs, injection molding guide and complex features](https://www.protolabs.com/resources/guides-and-trend-reports/injection-molding-guide-process-design-tips-materials/).
