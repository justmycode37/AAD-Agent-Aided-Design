# Hinges

Unmeasured candidate guidance. Choose the mechanism first: pin hinge, flexure/living hinge, or a purchased hinge. Specify axis, range, stops, retention, material, process, required cycles, and assembly method.

For a pin hinge, align the knuckle axes and check pin clearance, alternating knuckle order, retention, and the full swept envelope. For a living hinge, material, process, thickness transition, flow/orientation, bend radius, and cycle target matter; do not transfer one printer or resin guide to another.

Current AIAD builds a single 3D shape, not a moving assembly or motion proof. A static render cannot show binding or cycle life. Prototype and test the actual hinge.

Sources: [Protolabs, living hinges for molding](https://www.protolabs.com/resources/design-tips/designing-living-hinges-that-fold-flat/) and [Protolabs, 3D-printed living hinges](https://www.protolabs.com/resources/design-tips/how-to-design-3d-printed-hinges/).
